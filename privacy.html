Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains what the Trade99 Discord server and its bot, ClaudetteTradeBot (the "Service"), do with information.

1. What we collect

The bot does not read or store the content of your messages, and it does not ask for personal details such as your name, email or financial information. It posts information into the server and creates server events. If bot commands are added later, then when you use a command the bot receives your Discord user ID and the text of that command so it can respond. Discord itself handles your account data under its own Privacy Policy.

2. How we use it

Only to run the Service: respond to commands, post updates, keep the bot reliable, and prevent abuse. We keep short operational logs (such as errors and timestamps) for troubleshooting.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. The bot uses third-party services (for example Discord, and news and market-data providers) to do its job. Those services receive search queries and requests from the bot, not your personal information.

4. Retention

Operational logs are kept only as long as needed for maintenance and are deleted periodically. Anything you post in the server is stored by Discord under Discord's rules.

5. Security

We store credentials in a dedicated secrets manager and limit the bot's permissions to what it needs. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Age

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Your choices

You can leave the server at any time. To ask what information related to you we hold, or to have it deleted, contact an administrator of the Trade99 Discord server.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact an administrator of the Trade99 Discord server.
